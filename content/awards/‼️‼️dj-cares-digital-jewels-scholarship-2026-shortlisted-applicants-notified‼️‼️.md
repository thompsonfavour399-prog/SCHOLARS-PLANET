What Happens After Shortlisting?



According to the scholarship information published by Digital Jewels, shortlisted candidates may proceed to a panel interview before the final award decisions are made.



If you have been shortlisted, here are the next steps to take:



1. Check your email carefully: Look for the official shortlisting notification and read all instructions provided.

2. Review your interview details: If you have been invited for an interview, confirm the date, time, format, and any preparation requirements.

3. Prepare for the interview: Be ready to discuss your academic performance, career ambitions, interest in technology, financial need, and how the scholarship would support your educational goals.

4. Organise your documents: Keep your student identification card, proof of enrolment, academic records, and other requested documents ready.

5. Meet all deadlines: Submit any requested documents and respond to the scholarship organisers within the specified timeframe.

6. Await the final decision: Shortlisting is not the same as receiving the scholarship. Successful candidates should wait for an official award notification and follow the instructions provided.



 Benefits of the DJ CARES Digital Jewels Scholarship 2026



The DJ CARES Scholarship is an initiative of Digital Jewels designed to support academically strong Nigerian undergraduates with financial need who are pursuing eligible technology-related disciplines.



According to the official scholarship information, successful scholars may benefit from the following:



 1. Full University Tuition Coverage



The scholarship provides  100% coverage of university tuition fees from the time the award is granted until the end of the recipient's study programme, subject to the scholarship's terms and conditions.



This support can help reduce the financial burden of university education and allow beneficiaries to concentrate on their studies.



2. Professional Mentorship



Scholars can benefit from professional guidance and 

mentorship intended to support their academic development, career planning, and preparation for the workplace.



3. Technology and Industry Exposure



The programme aims to expose students to technology professionals and industry practices, particularly in Information Technology Governance, Risk, and Compliance (IT GRC).



This exposure can help scholars develop a better understanding of industry expectations and potential career pathways.



4. Career Development Opportunities



Beyond tuition support, the scholarship is designed to help students build relevant knowledge, professional connections, and the skills needed to transition successfully from university into the workplace.



5. Potential Employment Opportunities



According to Digital Jewels, scholars may receive prioritised job placement opportunities after graduation at its offices in Lagos or Abuja.



This is a potential benefit and should not be interpreted as a guaranteed job offer. Employment opportunities remain subject to the programme's applicable terms and conditions.



Who Is Eligible for the DJ CARES Scholarship?



The published eligibility criteria for the programme include the following:



\- Nigerian citizenship.

\- Full-time enrolment at an accredited federal or state university in Nigeria.

\- Study in an eligible technology-related discipline, including Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, Cybersecurity, Mechatronics, or Electrical Engineering.

\- Current study at the specified undergraduate level, generally 200 or 300 level.

\- A minimum CGPA of 3.5 on a 5.0 scale, or equivalent.

\- Demonstrated financial need.



Applicants should consult the official scholarship website for the complete eligibility criteria and the requirements applicable to the 2026 cohort.
