Applicants who applied for the NIPES Engineering and Science Scholarship 2026 are advised to check their email inboxes, including their spam and junk folders, for updates regarding their applications.



Shortlisted applicants have reportedly been contacted by the scholarship organisers.



Congratulations to all shortlisted applicants! We wish you success as you proceed to the next stage of the selection process.



**What Should Shortlisted Applicants Do Next?**



If you applied for the scholarship, take the following steps:



1. Check your email: Review your inbox, spam, junk, and promotions folders for an official notification from NIPES.

2. Read the instructions carefully: Check for deadlines, requests for additional documents, and any further selection requirements.

3. Prepare your documents: Keep your student identification card, proof of enrolment, academic records, and other relevant documents readily available.

4. Respond promptly: If you are required to confirm your interest or submit additional information, follow the instructions provided in your email.

5. Monitor official updates:Visit the NIPES website regularly for announcements concerning the scholarship.

6. Await final confirmation:Being shortlisted does not necessarily mean you have received the scholarship. Wait for an official award notification to confirm your final status.



**Benefits of the NIPES Engineering and Science Scholarship 2026**



The NIPES Engineering and Science Scholarship supports students pursuing engineering and science-related disciplines in higher institutions.



According to the published scholarship information, the programme features:



\- Total scholarship fund: ₦1,000,000.

\- Number of award recipients: Five students.

\- Individual scholarship award: ₦200,000 per recipient.

\- Financial assistance: Support towards the educational expenses of successful applicants.

\- Professional development: Potential opportunities to participate in NIPES activities and mentorship initiatives.

\- Student membership: Eligible non-members selected for the scholarship may receive a complimentary one-year NIPES student membership, subject to the programme's terms.



These benefits are subject to the official scholarship conditions and confirmation by NIPES.



 **Why the NIPES Scholarship Matters**

The cost of higher education can be a significant challenge for many students. Scholarship opportunities such as this can help ease some of the financial burden while encouraging academic excellence and professional growth.



For students studying engineering and science, the programme also provides an opportunity to connect with a professional community and strengthen their preparation for future careers in science, engineering, and technology.



How to Get More Information

Applicants should consult the official NIPES website for more info.
