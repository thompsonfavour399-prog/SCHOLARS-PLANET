**Organizer:** Female Scholars Foundation (FSF), Lagos, Nigeria

**Who Can Apply?**

The scholarship is open to young Nigerian women who meet all of the following:
Female, aged 20 or younger
Graduate of a public secondary school
Admitted into a public university
Minimum JAMB score of 240 in the 2026 UTME
At least five distinctions (A1–B3) in WAEC or NECO

The Foundation focuses on young women from rural communities. Selection is merit-based, and the application is completely free.

**Required Documents:**

WAEC, NECO or GCE results (WAEC is preferred, but NECO is accepted)
JAMB result showing at least 240
Proof of admission, such as a screenshot of your JAMB admission status (successful scholars later provide the original admission letter)
Completed application form
Motivation essay

**Selection Process:**

Applicants submit their academic information, documents and motivation essay. Shortlisted candidates are contacted by email or phone and interviewed online.

**Benefits:**

Full tuition fees throughout the university programme
Annual accommodation allowance
Annual textbook allowance
Monthly stipend
Mentorship and networking opportunities
International and regional internship opportunities
Internship and job opportunities after graduation
Private coaching, webinars and group retreats
Career guidance from industry experts

**Important Information:**

Rejected applicants cannot reapply for the same academic year.
Recipients receive an award letter stating the conditions for keeping the scholarship, including academic performance and participation in mentorship programmes.
If you already hold another scholarship, you must disclose it.

**Application Deadline:** 31 December 2026

**Application link:** https://femalescholars.org/
