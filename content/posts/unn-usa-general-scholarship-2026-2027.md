**UNN-USA General Scholarship 2026/2027**

The University of Nigeria Alumni Association, USA (UNN-USA) is pleased to announce the opening of applications for its General Scholarship for the 2026/2027 academic session.

**Eligibility Criteria**

To be considered for this scholarship, applicants must meet the following criteria:

1. Must be an active student of the University of Nigeria (UNN).
2. Awards are primarily merit-based.
3. Extenuating circumstances may also be considered where applicable.

**Application Requirements**

1. Complete the online scholarship application form via the official portal.
2. Applicants may upload supporting documents they would like the scholarship committee to consider.
3. Preferred document formats are PDF, PNG, or JPEG.

**Scholarship Benefits**

Successful applicants will receive a financial scholarship award. Previous awards have supported students across various faculties, including students with disabilities.

**Application Deadline**

October 1, 2026.

**How to Apply**

Interested and qualified candidates should apply through the official application link below:
