📍𝗘𝗟𝗜𝗚𝗜𝗕𝗜𝗟𝗜𝗧𝗬

• Must be an indigene of Ibeno Local Government Area, Akwa Ibom State.

• Must be studying in an accredited tertiary institution in Nigeria.

• Students in universities, polytechnics, colleges of education and other approved tertiary institutions are eligible.

• Both fresh and returning students can apply.



📍𝗥𝗘𝗤𝗨𝗜𝗥𝗘𝗠𝗘𝗡𝗧𝗦

𝗙𝗼𝗿 𝗙𝗿𝗲𝘀𝗵 𝗦𝘁𝘂𝗱𝗲𝗻𝘁𝘀:

• Certificate of Origin

• SSCE/WAEC/NECO or relevant school certificate

• JAMB or school admission letter

• Acceptance fee receipt



𝗙𝗼𝗿 𝗥𝗲𝘁𝘂𝗿𝗻𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗦𝘁𝘂𝗱𝗲𝗻𝘁𝘀:

• Certificate of Origin

• Previous school-fee receipts

• Confirmation letter from faculty or registrar

• Current academic CGPA

• School identification card



📍𝗕𝗘𝗡𝗘𝗙𝗜𝗧𝗦

• Scholarship support towards tertiary education.

• Available to both fresh and returning students.

• Covers eligible students studying across Nigeria.

30th November 2026
