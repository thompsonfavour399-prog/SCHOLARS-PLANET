📍ELIGIBILITY:

• Open to indigenes of Ukawu, Onicha LGA, Ebonyi State from financially disadvantaged backgrounds.

• Applicants must have gained admission into a recognised Nigerian university and must be entering university for the first time.

• Applicants must be 25 years old or younger at the time of application.

• Applicants must have at least five WAEC/NECO credits and must have written JAMB and met the required cut-off for admission.

• Applicants must be unmarried and must never have previously enrolled in a post-secondary institution.



📍SCHOLARSHIP BENEFITS:

💰 Successful scholars will receive ₦500,000 every year for the official duration of their university programme.

🏆 Students who graduate with a First Class will also receive a ₦1,000,000 graduation incentive.

🎓 Scholars will also have access to mentorship, leadership development and structured community-service opportunities.



📍REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:

• WAEC/NECO result

• JAMB result slip

• University admission letter

• Passport photograph

• Reference letter from a Ward Councillor or Community Leader



📍NUMBER OF SCHOLARS: 3 newly admitted students will be selected for the 2026 scholarship cycle.



📍APPLICATION DEADLINE: 30TH OCTOBER 2026
