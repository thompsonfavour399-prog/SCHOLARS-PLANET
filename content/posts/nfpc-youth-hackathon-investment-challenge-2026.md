**NFPC YOUTH HACKATHON & INVESTMENT CHALLENGE 2026**

Are you a young Nigerian passionate about innovation, family planning, health, SRHR and creating solutions to challenges affecting young people?

The Nigeria Family Planning Conference (NFPC) Youth Hackathon & Investment Challenge 2026 is calling for innovative ideas and solutions from young Nigerians.

**ORGANISER**:

Nigeria Family Planning Conference (NFPC)

**ELIGIBILITY**:

 Open to young Nigerians.

 Applicants should be affiliated with a CSO or enrolled in a higher institution.

 Individuals or teams of up to 3 people can participate.

 Applicants should have an interest or experience in family planning, SRHR or related areas.

**PRIZE POOL**:

 1st Place — ₦1,000,000 + 6-month mentorship

 2nd Place — ₦500,000 + 3-month mentorship

 3rd Place — ₦300,000

 Finalists also receive recognition, media exposure and networking opportunities.

**DEADLINE**:

15th October 2026

HOW TO APPLY:

Prepare and submit your concept note through the official NFPC application platform.

**APPLICATION LINK**:

<https://www.nfpc2026.org/youth-scholarship>

Do you have an innovative idea that can contribute to better health outcomes for young people? This could be your opportunity to develop it, present it and receive support.
