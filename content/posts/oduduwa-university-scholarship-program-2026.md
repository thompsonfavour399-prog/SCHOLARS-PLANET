**NEW SCHOLARSHIP OPPORTUNITY**

**ODUDUWA UNIVERSITY SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM 2026**

**FOCUS**: UNDERGRADUATE SCHOLARSHIP — OUI

**DETAILS**:
The Oduduwa University Scholarship Program is an opportunity for currently registered undergraduate students of \*Oduduwa University, Ipetumodu (OUI)\* who maintain good academic standing.

**ELIGIBILITY**:
• Must be a currently registered undergraduate student of Oduduwa University, Ipetumodu.
• Must be maintaining good academic standing.
• Must provide valid student registration and matriculation details.
• Applicants should use their active institutional details when completing the application.

**REQUIREMENTS**:
• Student registration details
• Matriculation details
• Required academic documentation

**APPLICATION DEADLINE**:
9th October 2026

**HOW TO APPLY**:
Complete the Google Form and provide the required institutional and academic information.

**APPLICATION LINK**:
<https://forms.gle/m43vAruapgzsnA8A>

**IMPORTANT**:
Ensure that the information provided is accurate and that all required academic documents are uploaded before submitting your application.
