PUNOCRACY PRIZE FOR SATIRE 2026

Are you a young Nigerian who loves writing, satire, humour, social commentary and creative expression?

The Punocracy Prize for Satire 2026 is now open for entries!

**ORGANISER**: Punocracy

**ELIGIBILITY**:

 Open to Nigerians aged 15–30 by December 2026.

 Your entry must be an original, unpublished work.

 Entries must be written in English.

 AI-generated submissions are not eligible.

**PRIZES**:

 1st Prize — ₦500,000

2nd Prize — ₦200,000

3rd Prize — ₦150,000

All entrants receive certificates, while selected longlisted works may be published.

**DEADLINE**:

31st October 2026

11:59 PM WAT

**HOW TO APPLY**:

Submit your entry through the official Punocracy Prize platform.

**APPLICATION LINK**:

<https://punocracy.com/prize/>

If you have a sharp pen, a creative mind and a story worth telling, this is an opportunity to put your writing to work.
