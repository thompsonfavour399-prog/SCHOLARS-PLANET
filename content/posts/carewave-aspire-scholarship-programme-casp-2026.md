**Organizer:** Carewave Foundation

**Who Can Apply?**

The scholarship is open to Nigerian undergraduate students who combine strong academic performance with genuine financial need. Applicants must:
Be a Nigerian citizen aged 18 to 25
Have secured admission into an eligible Federal University in Nigeria
Come from a vulnerable or financially disadvantaged background
Have a minimum GPA of 4.5 on a 5.0 scale
Demonstrate integrity, leadership potential and commitment to their education

**Eligible Courses:**

The scholarship covers a broad range of disciplines, including:
Health and medical programmes such as Medicine and Surgery, Nursing Science, Pharmacy, Medical Laboratory Science, Physiotherapy and Radiography
Technology and sciences such as Computer Science, Software Engineering, Cybersecurity, Information Technology, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Biotechnology, Microbiology, Biochemistry, Mathematics and Statistics
Engineering disciplines such as Electrical and Electronic, Mechanical, Civil, Chemical, Petroleum, Mechatronics, Agricultural and Environmental Engineering
Humanities and Social Sciences such as Law, Economics, Accounting, Finance, Mass Communication, Psychology, Political Science, Public Administration, Sociology, Business Administration, International Relations, Geography, Linguistics, Philosophy, History and Entrepreneurship

**Required Documents:**

Admission letter
JAMB admission letter
JAMB result slip
WAEC or NECO result
Birth certificate
Local Government identification
NIMC slip
Two letters of recommendation
Motivational letter
Proof of financial need

Proof of financial need may include a parent or guardian's payslip, household income evidence, bank statements, or a letter from a recognised community, religious or educational leader confirming financial hardship. Students who have lost one or both parents may provide supporting documentation.

**Benefits:**

₦500,000 per year for selected students
The award is for one academic year and can be renewed for students who keep meeting the academic and other requirements
Renewal depends on good academic standing, good conduct, continued enrolment in the approved programme, and submission of academic transcripts or progress reports
Scholars may also take part in Carewave mentorship and scholar activities

**How To Apply:**

Confirm that you meet the academic, admission and financial need requirements
Create or update your profile on the Carewave Foundation scholarship platform
Select the scholarship programme and complete the online application form
Upload all required documents
Review your information carefully and submit, as incomplete applications may not be considered

**Application Deadline:** 9 November 2026
