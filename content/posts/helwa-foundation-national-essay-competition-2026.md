**HELWA FOUNDATION NATIONAL ESSAY COMPETITION 2026**

**FOCUS**:
National Essay Competition for Female Undergraduates to elevate young female voices in leadership.

**THEME**:
"If I Were President for a Day"
If you had 24 hours as President, what ONE problem would you solve? Share an idea that empowers women and girls whilst creating positive change for everyone.

**BENEFITS**:
 Total Prize Pool: ₦1,000,000
 1st Place: ₦500,000
 2nd Place: ₦300,000
 3rd Place: ₦200,000

**ELIGIBILITY**:
- Open exclusively to female students aged 18-25
- Must be currently enrolled in a Nigerian tertiary institution

- Applications Open: October 1st, 2026
- Application Deadline: Not officially specified (Apply Immediately!)
