**Organizer**: Muslim Scholarship Fund Nigeria (MSFN)

**Who Can Apply?**

The scholarship is for academically capable but financially disadvantaged Nigerian Muslim students at the undergraduate and senior secondary levels. Applicants must:
Be Muslim
Be an indigene of one of the eligible states: Edo, Ekiti, Kogi, Kwara, Lagos, Ogun, Ondo, Osun or Oyo
Be currently enrolled in a public secondary school or tertiary institution
Be an active member of an Islamic organisation, such as the Muslim Students' Society of Nigeria (MSSN), a local mosque or a recognised Islamic or Da'wah organisation
Have credit passes in at least five subjects, including English Language and Mathematics
Come from a low-income family

Membership of MSSN is not compulsory, but participation in community activities is considered during selection.

**How To Apply:**

Visit the MSFN application portal
Create an account (or sign in if you already have one)
Complete the application form with accurate information
Upload the required supporting documents
Review your information and submit before the deadline

**Benefits:**

₦120,000 per year for undergraduate students
₦40,000 per year for senior secondary school students

Amounts may be reviewed annually depending on economic conditions.

**Application Period:** 22 September to 7 October 2026

**Application Deadline:** 7 October 2026
